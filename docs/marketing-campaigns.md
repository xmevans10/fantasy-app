# Primary marketing campaigns (drafted 2026-07-31)

Two headline campaigns, plus lighter recurring templates for the smaller seasonal moments.
Scoped for a solo-founder, zero-paid-budget push, following the same rule as `MARKETING.md`:
every plan states what actually has to get made and the one number that tells you it worked.
No mascot. Voice is the brand voice in `marketing-content-drafts.md`'s calibration section,
once locked.

---

## Campaign 1 — Debut

**Objective:** seed the first real follower base and establish the voice from post #1, not a
"new account, please be nice" soft launch. Target: enough initial engagement that the account
isn't posting into a void when the NFL kickoff campaign hits in about 5 weeks.

**Channels:** X + Reddit simultaneously (same week). TikTok deferred — no posting API access
yet, and the format needs a filmed board anyway (see the play-along script already drafted).

**Content needed:**
- Pinned debut post (X) — sample 5 in the voice-calibration section is the draft.
- A 4-6 image thread/carousel of real screenshots: Grid immaculate result (the flex screen —
  same slot-2 logic the App Store screenshots already use), Home daily pager, a Keep4 board,
  the crowd-rarity recap. Reuse assets from the ASC screenshot set if the aspect ratio works;
  otherwise a fresh simulator capture is cheap.
- Reddit r/SideProject launch story (drafted already) + r/apple weekly-app-thread post,
  posted same week as the X debut so anyone who clicks through sees an account already live.
- Bio/profile copy in-voice, short (something like "Nine cells, one guess each. New board
  daily."), profile picture (app icon is fine to start).

**Timing:** as soon as X + Reddit accounts and keys exist — don't wait for kickoff. Better to
have a few weeks of posting history and a small real following before the season's biggest
traffic spike than to debut cold into it.

**Success metric:** the MARKETING.md KPI already defined — share-grid posts from accounts
that aren't ours, >10/week, is the real signal; for the debut specifically, treat "the r/
SideProject post clears 50 upvotes without getting removed" as the go/no-go read on whether
the story itself lands.

## Campaign 2 — NFL Kickoff ("the moment")

**Objective:** convert the single biggest seasonal spike in sports-app search/install intent
into actual installs, timed to the NFL season opener (~early Sept, ~5 weeks out as of this
writing). This is the campaign every other plan in `MARKETING.md` treats as rehearsal for.

**Content needed:**
- App Store: kickoff-week promo text rotation (mechanical — same ASC API path already used
  for the training-camp line) + a seasonal screenshot slot swap (Home showing the NFL daily
  card front and center).
- X: a full week of daily posts (board post + at least one Ref bit/day), one big engagement
  post day-of-kickoff ("who ya got" / bold-prediction bait that invites replies), a preview
  post the week before ("training camp's over, here's what's coming").
  - Rotate promotional text with the sports calendar, timed against the actual schedule
    release / opener date — MARKETING.md's existing cadence idea.
- Reddit: one themed r/fantasyfootball post ("Keep4's rank-these-RB-seasons format maps onto
  draft-prep arguments") during the week draft-prep chatter peaks, not kickoff week itself.
- Creator outreach: 2-3 mid-size NFL-trivia TikTokers, collab/shoutout ask. This one's a
  [user] item — outreach DMs to real people are outside the "no DM autonomy" boundary we set,
  so I'll draft the outreach message but you send it.

**Timing:** hype-week content starts ~1 week before kickoff; the big daily-posting push is
kickoff week itself and the following 2-3 weeks (early-season is still high-intent).

**Success metric:** App Store page conversion rate (Analytics → App Store) before/after the
promo rotation, same KPI `MARKETING.md` §4 already defined; installs attributed to the
link-in-bio, day-over-day, during kickoff week specifically.

## Recurring template — single-sport seasonal moments

Lighter than a full campaign: NBA opening night (Oct), MLB postseason, a tennis major final,
a big soccer transfer-window move. Each gets the same skeleton, scaled down:
- One hype post the day before/of, one board-post-plus-Ref-bit the day itself.
- No dedicated App Store rotation unless the moment is genuinely major (kickoff-scale) —
  most of these fold into the always-on trend-reactive engine rather than a standalone push.
- No creator outreach unless a specific moment clearly warrants it (case-by-case, not default).

## Deferred — Pro/monetization push

Not scoped as its own campaign yet, deliberately: marketing a subscription before there's a
habituated free-tier audience is backwards. Revisit once the debut + kickoff campaigns have
actually built a following — the natural moment is once daily-active users exist to convert,
not before.
