Why it's hard

The stat line is right there. It still isn't enough.

Every card shows the full box score. What it hides is the grade — the same fantasy-points math for every player, so a 1975 season and a 2015 season can be ranked against each other honestly. Era adjustment is what makes Marino's 44 touchdowns a cut and a 1970s workhorse a keep.

No trivia questionsNothing comes from a quiz bank. Every card is a real season pulled from real box-score data.
One grading ruleThe same scoring formula grades every season in a sport. You are never guessing what the app "meant".
Hard mode hides the statsName, team, year. That is all you get. It is the version people send to the group chat.
A new board every dayBoards are minted nightly and never repeat — once a puzzle has been the daily, it is retired for good.

Every grade is revealed at the end, next to what the board's real top four turned out to be.
