## Recurring post formats

Things that can run weekly without new design work:

1. **Board of the day** — screenshot today's K4C4 board, caption "sort these", answer in replies
   the next day. The single highest-engagement format for this kind of app.
2. **Journeyman crest timeline** — post the clubs only. Reveals are perfect reply bait, and the
   board is already visually self-contained.
3. **Blitz run** — screen recording of a 1-minute run with the score at the end.
4. **Stat of the week** — one genuinely surprising season the catalog surfaced.
5. **Streak milestones** — user-generated; reshare with permission.

## Legal / claims

- **Never imply league affiliation.** Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL, NBA, MLB,
  or any club. If a post could read as official, it needs rewording.
- **Team crests and player photos** appear inside the app under the same terms the app ships
  under; that isn't a licence to build standalone marketing art around a club badge. Post
  screenshots of the app, not extracted logos.
- **Don't use "Immaculate Grid"** in marketing copy — it's a Sports Reference product name. The
  app's own perfect-board wording should be reviewed for the same reason.
- **Every number must be true.** "380,000+ player-seasons" is checkable; keep it checkable.
